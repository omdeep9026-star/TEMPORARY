Act 01 · camera scrub

Scroll is the only timeline.

A single Three.js camera on a fixed stage. Its position and target are pure functions of scroll progress, damped toward the target every frame so wheel notches become a glide.

Techniques: scroll → camera Z with bounded travel · exponential playhead damping · velocity-driven chromatic spread · fog as depth cue.

Act 02 · pinned + scrubbed cues

One stage.
Four arguments.
All on the same frame.

Cue windows overlap so the stage is never empty; the last cue holds so the act ends on a statement.

Act 03 · canvas frame sequence

A flip-book drawn from a number

120 procedural frames. The scroll position picks the frame, a lerped playhead eases toward it, and each frame is a pure function of its index — so scrubbing backwards is exact.

FRAME 000 / 120

A frame already on the canvas is not redrawn, and the canvas is only touched while the act is on screen.

Act 04 · layered parallax + focus pull

Depth is differential motion

Five planes, each with its own rate. The plane of sharp focus travels from the far ridge to the foreground, and every other plane is blurred by its distance from it.

Blur is quantised to 0.5 px steps so the compositor reuses the rasterised layer instead of re-filtering five full-bleed planes on every frame.

Act 05 · cinematic transition

not on a timer.

The veil is driven by section progress, so the cut lands exactly where the reader put it and reverses perfectly on the way back up.
